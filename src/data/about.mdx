I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at **Georgia State University**, advised by **Dr. Xiaojun Cao**. My research focuses on resource-aware deployment and optimization of network and AI services over distributed infrastructures.

My research interests include **service function chaining and network function virtualization**, **distributed large language model inference**, **mixture-of-experts deployment**, and **agentic AI workflow mapping**. I develop optimization algorithms and learning-based approaches for efficient, reliable, and security-aware service deployment in edge and distributed networks.

I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from **Southwest Jiaotong University** in 2024.
